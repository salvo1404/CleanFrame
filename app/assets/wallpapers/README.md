# Launcher wallpapers

The 24 entries in `SOURCES.json` (the original fourteen plus 10 Australian nature photographs from Wikimedia Commons, marked `nature`). An optional `place` field, taken from the photo's source page, drives the Home caption. each have a background JPEG, a thumbnail and a small blurred panel backdrop. Four registered material JPEGs per image live in `../materials/`. All derived images retain the source photograph's terms.

Photo pages, authors and licence observations are in [asset sources](../../../docs/asset-sources.md). Generation instructions are in [asset generation](../../../docs/assets.md). Full-size originals remain outside the repository.
